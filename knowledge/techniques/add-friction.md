# Add friction, remove friction

Tags: friction, too easy, autopilot, habit, phone, logged in, one tap, environment design

## When it helps
Scrolling wins because it takes one tap and work takes ten. Behavior follows the path of least effort, so change the effort instead of relying on willpower in the moment.

## How to do it
Make the distraction slower: log out of the site, remove it from bookmarks and the browser start page, keep your phone in another room while you work. Make the good option faster: leave tomorrow's task open in a tab, keep your notes or book on the desk.
