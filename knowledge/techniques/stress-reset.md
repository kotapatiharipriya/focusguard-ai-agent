# A two-minute stress reset

Tags: stress, anxiety, anxious, overwhelmed, worried, tense, nervous, panic

## When it helps
You open feeds when you feel stressed or anxious. The scroll numbs the feeling for a moment, but it doesn't settle it, and the time lost often adds to the stress.

## How to do it
Stop and breathe out slowly, longer than you breathe in, five or six times. Name what's bothering you in one sentence, on paper if you can. Then pick the smallest action that moves it forward, or decide when you'll deal with it. Naming a worry and giving it a time makes it lighter than avoiding it.
