# When you're tired, rest for real

Tags: tired, exhausted, low energy, burnt out, need a break, drained

## When it helps
Scrolling feels like rest but usually isn't: it keeps your eyes and attention busy and leaves you as tired or more. If you open feeds because you're drained, what you need is recovery, not more input.

## How to do it
Try ten minutes of something that actually restores you: lie down with eyes closed, walk outside without your phone, drink water and eat something, stretch, or take a short nap before 3 PM. Then decide whether to work or to stop for the day.

## Try this in FocusGuard AI
Use a break instead of a focus session: the timer still runs, the feeds stay closed, and you get the rest you were looking for.
