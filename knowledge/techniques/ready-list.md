# Keep a ready list for boredom

Tags: bored, boredom, nothing to do, waiting, empty time, restless, short break

## When it helps
You open feeds when you're bored or between things. Boredom is a real signal that you want stimulation or meaning; the feed answers it in the cheapest way and leaves you feeling flat.

## How to do it
Keep a short list of things you actually enjoy that take five to fifteen minutes: a walk around the block, a song on an instrument, a chapter of a book, sketching, texting a friend you miss, a short workout. When boredom hits, pick from the list instead of deciding from scratch.

## Try this in FocusGuard AI
Add two or three of these as tasks. When a blocked site sends you to the Focus page out of boredom, do one of them.
