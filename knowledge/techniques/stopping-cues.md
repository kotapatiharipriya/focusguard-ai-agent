# Give yourself stopping cues

Tags: infinite scroll, endless feed, lost track of time, shorts, reels, swiping, no end

## When it helps
Books have chapters and episodes have endings. Infinite feeds and short-video swiping have no natural stopping point, so you only stop when something outside interrupts you, often much later than you meant to.

## How to do it
Decide the end before you start: a number of minutes, a number of videos, or an event ("until the kettle boils"). Set a timer you can hear. When it goes off, stand up physically; changing posture breaks the trance better than willpower.
