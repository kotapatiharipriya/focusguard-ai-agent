# After a slip

Tags: worse, guilt, failed, relapse, bad day, ashamed, all or nothing

## When it helps
You lost an hour or a whole evening and feel bad about it. Harsh self-criticism feels responsible but tends to make the next slip more likely, because feeling bad is itself a trigger for escape.

## How to do it
Treat it as data, not a verdict. Ask: what was the trigger, what time was it, what was I avoiding or needing? Then make one small adjustment, like a new if-then plan or a stricter mode for one site. Progress is fewer and shorter slips, not zero.
