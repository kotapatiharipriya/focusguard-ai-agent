# Urge surfing

Tags: urge, craving, reopening, compulsive, checking, keep coming back, habit loop

## When it helps
You close the app and reopen it a minute later without deciding to. The pull feels urgent, but urges rise, peak and fade like a wave, usually within a few minutes, if you don't feed them.

## How to do it
When the pull comes, don't fight it and don't obey it. Notice where you feel it (hands, chest, restlessness), name it ("this is the urge to check"), and breathe slowly while you watch it for two or three minutes. Each time you let an urge pass without acting, the next one gets a little weaker.
