# An evening wind-down that isn't a feed

Tags: late night, bed, sleep, night, 10 pm, 11 pm, midnight, evening, revenge bedtime procrastination

## When it helps
Late-night scrolling often comes from wanting time that feels like your own after a long day. It steals sleep, and tired days lead to even more scrolling the next night.

## How to do it
Pick a screens-off time and charge your phone outside the bedroom. Replace the feed with a short routine you look forward to: a shower, tea, a paper book, stretching, writing tomorrow's top three tasks. Protect real me-time earlier in the evening so you don't have to steal it at midnight.
