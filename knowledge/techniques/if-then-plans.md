# If-then plans

Tags: late night, evening, routine, trigger, habit, same time every day, implementation intention

## When it helps
Your scrolling happens at predictable moments: right after dinner, in bed, when you sit down to work. A general intention ("I'll scroll less") loses to a specific cue every time. A plan that names the cue in advance gives your brain a ready-made response when the moment arrives.

## How to do it
Write one sentence in the form "If [situation], then I will [specific action]." Make the situation concrete and the action small. Examples: "If it's 9:30 PM and I'm on the couch, then I start a 25-minute focus session on tomorrow's first task." "If I open YouTube on my laptop, then I first write the one video I came for."

## Try this in FocusGuard AI
Put your if-then plan at the top of your task list so the Focus page shows it every time a blocked site sends you there. If your drift clusters at one hour, start a focus session a few minutes before that hour.
