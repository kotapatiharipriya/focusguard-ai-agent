# Check on purpose, not on impulse

Tags: fomo, missing out, news, updates, notifications, checking, what's happening

## When it helps
You keep checking feeds so you don't miss anything. Each check feels small, but dozens of them break your focus all day and rarely give you anything you needed right then.

## How to do it
Pick two or three set times a day to catch up, for example after lunch and early evening, and do it fully then. Turn off non-essential notifications. When the urge comes between those times, remind yourself the next check is already scheduled.
