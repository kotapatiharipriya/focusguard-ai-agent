# The five-minute start

Tags: avoiding, procrastination, stuck, task too big, overwhelmed, don't know where to start, starting

## When it helps
You reach for the feed when a task feels big, vague or uncomfortable. Scrolling isn't the problem then; it's an escape from the feeling the task gives you. The hardest part of most tasks is the first few minutes.

## How to do it
Agree to work on the task for just five minutes, and make the first step tiny and visible: open the document and write one bad sentence, list three bullet points, read one page. Permission to stop after five minutes removes the pressure. Most of the time, you'll keep going.

## Try this in FocusGuard AI
Use "Break down" on a task that keeps getting skipped and start a focus session on the first step only. A finished small step beats an untouched big task.
